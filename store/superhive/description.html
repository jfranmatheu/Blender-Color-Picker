🎨 Pick colors without breaking your flow

Every time you change color while painting, you reach for the header, hunt for the color swatch, pick, and go back to your stroke. Do that a few hundred times a session and it adds up.

Color Picker puts the picker where your eyes already are: right under your mouse. Hold Space, pick, release. You never leave the viewport and you never lose your place.

It works in Texture Paint, Vertex Paint and Weight Paint, and it is fully updated for Blender 5.

⚡ Hold. Pick. Release.

  1. Hold Space over the 3D Viewport. The picker pops up under your cursor.
  2. Click or drag to choose a color. Your brush updates instantly.
  3. Release Space and keep painting. That's it.

Prefer a toggle? Turn off Close on Hot-key Release and the picker stays open until you press the key again, click outside it or press Esc.

🌈 Three layouts. Pick the one that fits your hand.

  • SV Square – the classic: saturation and value in one square, hue on a slider. Fast and familiar.
  • SV Square + Hue Ring – everything in one gesture: spin the ring for hue, drag in the square for tone. No sliders needed.
  • HS Color Wheel – the painter's wheel: angle for hue, distance for saturation, with a value slider for brightness.

Every layout comes with live H / S / V and R / G / B sliders. Each slider shows a gradient of the colors you'd get by moving it, so you see the result before you drag.

✨ Everything you need, nothing in your way

  • 🖌️ Texture, Vertex and Weight Paint – one tool for all your paint modes.
  • ⚖️ Weight bar – in Weight Paint, a 0.0 to 1.0 gradient bar right under your cursor.
  • 🔗 Unified Color aware – edits the shared color or the active brush, exactly like Blender does.
  • #️⃣ Hex code – show the current color as a hex value, handy for matching references.
  • ⌨️ Your hotkey – rebind the key separately for each paint mode.
  • 🔍 Your size – scale the picker for high-resolution screens.
  • 🧩 Native extension – installs like any Blender 5 extension, no setup required.

🔧 Compatibility & installation

  • Blender 5.0 and newer (tested in Blender 5.3).
  • Builds for Blender 2.93, 3.x and 4.x are available on GitHub.
  1. Download the zip.
  2. In Blender, open Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions, click the menu in the top-right corner and choose Install from Disk… (or just drag and drop the zip into Blender).
  3. Enter a paint mode, hover over the viewport and hold Space.
