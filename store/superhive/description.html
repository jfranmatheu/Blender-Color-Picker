A fast color picker that pops up right under your mouse in Texture Paint, Vertex Paint and Weight Paint. Hold Space, pick, release, and keep painting. Fully updated for Blender 5.

Compatibility

  • Blender 5.0 and newer (tested in Blender 5.3)
  • Texture Paint, Vertex Paint and Weight Paint
  • Builds for Blender 2.93, 3.x and 4.x on GitHub

📖 Documentation · ❓ FAQ

Color Picker is free and open source. You can download it from GitHub at any time. It's made by one independent developer, and buying it here is what keeps it updated for every new Blender version, and free for students and artists who can't pay for tools right now.

Thanks to your support, Color Picker stays open, free and public. ♥
