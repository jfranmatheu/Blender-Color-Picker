**🎨 What it does**

Hold **Space** in the 3D Viewport and a compact color picker pops up right under your mouse. Click or drag to pick a color, release to close, and keep painting. No more trips to the header or the sidebar.

- Works in **Texture Paint**, **Vertex Paint** and **Weight Paint**
- **Three layouts:** SV Square, SV Square + Hue Ring, and HS Color Wheel
- Live **H/S/V** and **R/G/B** sliders with gradient previews
- **Weight bar** from 0.0 to 1.0 in Weight Paint
- Respects Blender's **Unified Color** setting
- Optional **hex code** display
- Rebindable hotkey per paint mode, adjustable size

**🔧 Requirements**

- Blender **5.0 or newer** (tested in Blender 5.3)
- Builds for older Blender versions (2.93, 3.x, 4.x) are in the GitHub repository

**📦 Installation**

1. Download the zip (from GitHub or from this purchase).
2. In Blender, go to *Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions*, open the menu in the top-right corner and choose *Install from Disk…*, then select the zip. You can also drag and drop the zip into Blender.
3. Enter a paint mode, hover over the viewport and hold **Space**.
